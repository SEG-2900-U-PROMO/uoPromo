THE PEOPLE BEHIND UOPROMO

Meet Our Team

We are five first-year Software Engineering students at the University of Ottawa. Together, we are building UoPromo to make discovering and sharing student deals easier and more accessible.

By combining our ideas, HTML development, design and CSS skills, we are creating a platform that helps students connect with offers from local businesses and student associations.
